Privacy Policy

Data Protection and Usage

SABIQ is designed as a prototype to support decision-making through digital simulation and analysis of potential decision impacts.

The prototype platform uses simulated and anonymized data for simulation and analysis purposes. No real personal data is used.

The results are intended to help decision-makers understand potential scenarios and do not represent a guaranteed prediction of what will happen in reality.
